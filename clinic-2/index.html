Message us
Are all of your doctors women?

Yes. All fourteen doctors, every sonographer and every nurse at Rahma is a woman. Fathers are welcome to join appointments and scans.

Do you deliver babies here?

Rahma is an outpatient clinic. We look after you through pregnancy and after birth, and book your delivery at a partner hospital in Jeddah where your Rahma obstetrician holds privileges.

Can my child be seen today?

We keep same-day slots for sick children every afternoon and evening. Call or walk in before 9:30 pm and a paediatrician will see your child the same evening.

Which vaccines are free?

Vaccines on the national schedule are covered for Saudi citizens and by most insurance policies. Optional vaccines such as flu are priced at reception before the dose is given.

Can I choose my doctor?

Yes. Pick a doctor when you book and we keep you with her for the whole pregnancy, or for your child's checks, unless you ask to change.

Is there a prayer room and parking?

Free parking is under the building with a lift to every floor. There is a women's prayer room on level 2 and a private feeding room next to the children's clinic.
