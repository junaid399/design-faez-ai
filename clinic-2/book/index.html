Arriving for the first time.

  1. Park on B1. Free for patients, with wide bays for prams next to the lift.
  2. Take the lift to level 1. Reception is straight ahead, the play corner on your left.
  3. Come 10 minutes early. We take your ID, insurance card and, for children, the vaccination card.

Good to know before you come.

Changing or cancelling

Reply to your WhatsApp confirmation or call us. There is no fee, we only ask for a few hours' notice so another family can take the slot.

Insurance approval

Send a photo of your card when we call. Most approvals arrive in about 20 minutes.

Fathers and family

Welcome in consultations and scans. One adult at a time in the treatment rooms.

Prices without insurance

A first consultation is SAR 350, a follow up SAR 250 and a pregnancy scan from SAR 400. We confirm the full price before anything is done.
