عيادة الأطفال

Small patients, unhurried doctors.

Six women paediatricians for newborn checks, vaccines, growth and the coughs and fevers in between. Evenings included, because children rarely fall ill at a convenient time.

Feverish, coughing, covered in spots? Come in tonight.

We hold same-day slots for sick children every afternoon and evening. Walk in before 9:30 pm, no booking needed.

  1. 0 minArrive and register
  2. 10 minNurse checks temperature and breathing
  3. 30 minSeen by a paediatrician
  4. 45 minHome with a written plan

Trouble breathing, a seizure or a baby under 3 months with fever: call 997 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Your baby's first six weeks, step by step.

Five short visits that catch the things new parents worry about at 3 am. Mother and baby can be seen in the same room.

  1. Day 1 to 3

    Heel prick test

    Screens for 16 rare conditions, results by phone within a week.

  2. Day 3 to 5

    Jaundice check

    A quick skin light reading, no needle unless it is high.

  3. Day 7

    Weight and feeding

    With a lactation nurse, for breast or bottle.

  4. Day 14

    Back to birth weight

    Cord, skin and sleep questions answered.

  5. Week 6

    Full check

    Hips, eyes, heart and the plan for the two-month vaccines.

Three things that make the visit easier.

01

Bring the green vaccination card

We record every dose in Sehhaty too, but the card is what schools and travel clinics still ask for. We stamp it at every visit.

02

Pack their favourite toy

A familiar bear helps more than any distraction we can offer. The doctor will happily check its heartbeat first.

03

Tell us what scares them

Needles, white coats, the scale. Mention it at reception and we adjust: numbing cream, no coat, the play corner first.

Choose a paediatrician and keep her.

The same doctor from the first heel prick to the school entry check. She will know your child's history before you open your mouth.
