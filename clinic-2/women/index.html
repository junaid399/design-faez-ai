Pregnancy care متابعة الحمل

One obstetrician from your first visit to your six-week check. She books your delivery at a partner hospital and you keep her mobile number for the last month.

  • 12 antenatal visits with blood pressure, urine and growth checks
  • 4 scans: dating, nuchal, anomaly and growth
  • Gestational diabetes screening and Tdap vaccine at 28 weeks
  • Breastfeeding class with a lactation nurse at 34 weeks
First visit
30 min
Follow ups

Gynaecology أمراض النساء

Heavy or painful periods, PCOS, fibroids, menopause and screening. We explain each option, including doing nothing, before you decide.

  • Smear tests and HPV vaccination for women up to 45
  • Pelvic ultrasound in the same visit when needed
  • Hormone and thyroid bloods with results in 24 hours
  • Menopause plans reviewed every six months
First visit
30 min
Follow ups
20 min
Lead doctor
Dr. Mai Al Shehri

Fertility الخصوبة

For couples trying for a year, or six months if you are over 35. Both partners are tested in the first month and you leave with a written plan.

  • Day 2 hormones, AMH and tube test (HSG)
  • Semen analysis at our partner andrology lab
  • Follicle tracking scans and ovulation induction
  • IUI on site, IVF referral with your records ready
First visit
45 min, both partners
Lead doctor
Dr. Lama Al Harbi

Scans الأشعة الصوتية

Two scan rooms with large screens on the wall, so you see what the sonographer sees. Every report is signed by a fetal medicine doctor.

  • Pregnancy scans from 6 weeks, including 4D
  • Pelvic and follicle tracking scans
  • Images sent to your phone before you leave
  • Partners welcome in the room
Scan slot
20 to 40 min
Lead doctor
Dr. Sara Qadi

After birth ما بعد الولادة

The six weeks after birth get the least attention and need the most. We see mother and baby together, in the same room, on the same day.

  • Wound, blood pressure and bleeding checks
  • Breastfeeding support with a lactation nurse
  • Mood check for postnatal depression at every visit
  • Contraception and spacing advice at six weeks
Joint visit
40 min, mother and baby
Lead nurse
Nurse Rana Bawazir

The fertility pathway, step by step.

Most couples know their next step within four weeks. About 4 in 10 conceive within six months of their first visit, without needing IVF.

  1. 1Week 0

    First consultation

    History, cycle review and a plan for both partners.

  2. 2Weeks 1 to 4

    Tests

    Hormones on day 2, AMH, tube test and semen analysis.

  3. 3Months 1 to 3

    Tracking and induction

    Follicle scans and tablets to time ovulation.

  4. 4Months 3 to 6

    IUI

    Up to three cycles of insemination here at Rahma.

  5. 5If needed

    IVF referral

    To a partner centre in Jeddah, with your full file sent ahead.

4 in 10 couples conceive within six months of their first Rahma visit. Clinic figures for 2025, 312 couples.
