Hegra, the Kingdom'sfirst World Heritage site

Two thousand years ago this was the southern city of the Nabataeans, the same people who carved Petra. They left their dead in tombs cut from single outcrops, with the names of the people who paid for them written above the door.

The valley, in eight dates

  1. c. 600 BC

    Dadan and Lihyan

    Incense caravans make Dadan the capital of a kingdom that taxes the trade road north.

  2. 1st century AD

    The Nabataeans at Hegra

    Tombs are carved for merchant families, many with the year of the king's reign above the door.

  3. 106 AD

    Rome arrives

    The Nabataean kingdom is annexed and Hegra becomes Rome's furthest outpost in Arabia.

  4. 12th century

    A walled old town

    Mud brick houses rise on older stone, joined wall to wall for shade and defence.

  5. 1908

    The Hejaz Railway

    Steam trains stop at Hegra on the way to Madinah. The station still stands.

  6. 1983

    The last families leave

    The old town empties as families move to new houses across the oasis.

  7. 2008

    UNESCO listing

    Hegra becomes the first World Heritage Site in Saudi Arabia.

  8. 2017

    The valley opens

    A royal commission begins restoring sites and welcoming visitors year round.

Before you go

01

Dress for sun and stone

Loose clothes that cover shoulders and knees, closed shoes for the tombs and a layer for winter evenings, when it can drop to 6 °C.

02

Tickets are timed

Hegra and Dadan run on fixed slots that sell out in winter. Tell us your dates and we book them with your room.

03

Follow the shade

From May to September we plan outings before 10 am and after 4 pm. The middle of the day is for the pool and the hammam.
