How the houseis laid out

Rooms follow the curve of the cliff. Cars stop at the gate; from there it is footpaths, lanterns and a golf cart if you want one.

In every room,before you ask

Sleep that ignores the heat

Walls sixty centimetres thick and deep set windows keep rooms near 21 °C in August, with blackout shutters and cotton linen woven in Egypt.

Coffee corner

A dallah, Khawlani beans roasted in AlUla and a bowl of Barhi dates, refilled each evening.

Prayer mat and qibla

The direction is set into the floor in brass. Prayer times are printed by the door.

Night sky kit

Red torch, binoculars and a star chart for the month, so your eyes stay used to the dark.

Desert kit

Shemagh scarf, walking poles and a steel flask to borrow for Hegra and the trails.

Quiet tech

Fast Wi-Fi, no television unless you ask. Speakers come on a tray from the desk.
