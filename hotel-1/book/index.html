The small print,kept short

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 3 pm, check-out by 12 noon. Booking direct gives you check-out until 2 pm at no charge, and we hold bags and offer the spa showers if your flight is late.

Changing or cancelling

Change dates free of charge up to 7 days before arrival. Cancel up to 14 days before for a full refund; after that the first night is charged. Festive dates from 20 December to 5 January need 30 days' notice.

Paying

We send a secure payment link once you confirm the hold, or you can pay on arrival by card, mada or Apple Pay. Prices are in Saudi riyals and include VAT and the tourism fee.

Children

Children of every age are welcome. Under 6 stay and eat breakfast free; cots and child beds are free in Wadi Suites and Pool Villas. Pools are unfenced, so we ask that children are supervised.

Step free access

Four Canyon Rooms and two Wadi Suites are step free with roll-in showers, and the lantern path is paved end to end. Tell us what you need and we will match the room to it.

Airport transfers

Free from AlUla International for direct stays of two nights or more, otherwise SAR 220 each way. We track your flight, so delays are not a problem.
