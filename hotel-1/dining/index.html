Twenty eight minutesfor one pot of coffee

Saudi coffee is listed by UNESCO as living heritage. Ours is made the slow way at the terrace every morning, and you are welcome to stand at the fire and learn.

  1. 1
    Roast

    Khawlani beans roasted pale, almost golden

    12 min
  2. 2
    Grind

    By hand in a brass mihbash, with the rhythm of a song

    3 min
  3. 3
    Boil

    In the dallah with cardamom, a little saffron and clove

    10 min
  4. 4
    Settle and pour

    A palm fibre in the spout, poured from the right hand

    3 min

When you have had enough, give the small cup a gentle shake as you hand it back.
