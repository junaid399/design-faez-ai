Built from the cliffit sleeps beneath

The house sits where the wadi bends west, under a wall of Cambrian sandstone a hundred and forty metres high. Its walls are made of that same stone, cut and laid by masons from AlUla's old town, so in the late afternoon you cannot tell where the cliff ends and the house begins.

We kept the courtyards small and the windows deep, the way the old town did, and planted the gardens with sidr, moringa and date palms that need almost nothing. The result is a place that stays cool by design and quiet by default.

The sky AlUlakept dark

There are no streetlights in the wadi and the house is lit from below at ankle height. On a moonless night you see the Milky Way cast a faint shadow on the sand. Our astronomer, Faisal, sets up the telescope on the roof at 10 pm.
