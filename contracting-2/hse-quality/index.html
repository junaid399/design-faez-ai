A working day built around the Jeddah summer.

From mid-June to mid-September nobody works in direct sun between noon and three. We go further than the rule: large pours move to the night and every site measures heat stress hourly.

  • WBGT reading every hour posted at the gate and sent to every foreman's phone.
  • Cooling stations within 150 m of every work front, with chilled water and shade.
  • Buddy checks every 30 minutes above 32°C WBGT, and new arrivals acclimatise over 10 days.
