Before you ask.

Do you price design-build and build-only tenders?

Yes. About 40% of our current order book is design-build. For build-only tenders we price to your drawings and list any design gaps we find as clarifications.

What is the smallest package you take on?

Around SAR 40 million for buildings and civil works. Marine works start lower, at about SAR 15 million, because of our own plant.

Can you work inside a PMC's reporting system?

Yes. We currently report into three PMC platforms and issue Primavera P6 updates in XER format every week.

Where can you mobilise?

Anywhere in the Western Region and the north-west, from Taif to NEOM. Mobilisation is typically 21 days from award.

How do I get your prequalification pack?

Send the form above with "prequalification" in the message. The pack includes certificates, financials, HSE statistics and reference projects.
