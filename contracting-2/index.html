Six controls between tender and handover.

The method does not change with the size of the job. It is what lets a PMC compare our sites line by line.

  1. 01

    Day 0 to 10

    Tender review

    Quantities rechecked, method statements drafted and every risk priced, not buried in prelims.

  2. 02

    Day 21

    Mobilisation

    Site offices, batching and first crews in place three weeks after award.

  3. 03

    Week 4

    P6 baseline

    A resource-loaded baseline, typically 3,000 to 5,000 activities, approved with your planners.

  4. 04

    Daily

    Site record

    Drone pass, site diary and measured quantities logged before 10:00.

    Three-week plan, constraints log and S-curve issued every Thursday.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Close-out

    Testing, snag close-out, as-built models and a 12-month defects team on call.
