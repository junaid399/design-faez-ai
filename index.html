How the thirty were chosen

Registration data

Commercial-registration and licence counts from the Saudi Ministry of Commerce, GASTAT, REGA, the Contractors Authority, Qatar's MoCI open data, and Oman's NCSI and Riyada registers. Big, growing categories first.

Digital gap

Only 29% of Saudi establishments use the internet to sell or display their services. Most of these categories still run on Instagram and WhatsApp, which is exactly where a website pays back.

Ad economics

Each demo is the landing page for a country-level ad test. Niches with higher ticket sizes and clear licensing triggers, such as ZATCA e-invoicing or REGA broker licences, were ranked ahead of low-ticket services.
