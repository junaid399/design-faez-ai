  1. 01 / HVAC and chilled water

    Air first, because ducts need the most room.

    Air-cooled chillers and AHUs on the roof, a supply riser down the core, then main ducts and branches in the void. Every run is sized from the heat-load model for an Al Khobar July, not a catalogue table.

  2. 02 / Electrical and LV

    Power from the main board to the last downlight.

    Main LV board on the ground floor, busbar riser through the core, cable trays and lighting on every level. We build the boards in our Dammam workshop and test them before they reach site.

    Boards
    Form 4 type 2, IEC 61439
    Lighting
    DALI, 8 W/m² office
    Test
    IR, loop impedance, RCD

  3. 03 / Plumbing and drainage

    Water up, waste down, nothing in between that leaks.

    Roof tanks and a booster set feed copper and PPR risers to each wet core. Drainage runs on gravity wherever the slab allows, and every joint is pressure tested before a ceiling tile goes near it.

    Pressure test
    1.5 x working, 2 hours
    Hot water
    60 °C store, 50 °C at tap
    Code
    SBC 701 and 702

  4. 04 / Fire protection and alarm

    Sprinklers, alarm and pumps, signed off by Civil Defense.

    A diesel and electric fire pump set, a wet riser, sprinkler mains on every floor and heads dropped to the finished ceiling grid. The addressable alarm talks to the AHUs, dampers and lifts.

    Design
    NFPA 13, light hazard
    Coverage
    12 m² per head
    Alarm

Six weeks of testing before you get the keys.

Commissioning is where MEP contractors usually run out of time. We plan it from the first programme, with its own manager and its own test sheets, so the building works on day one rather than after the first summer.

  1. Week -6

    Pre-commissioning

    Static checks, flushing and pressure tests at 1.5 times working pressure.

  2. Week -4

    Energisation

    LV boards energised. Insulation and continuity results logged per circuit.

  3. Week -3

    Start-up

    Chillers, AHUs and pumps run with the manufacturers' engineers.

  4. Week -2

    Functional tests

    Every point checked against design intent on the BMS, room by room.

  5. Week -1

    Integrated test

    Fire alarm drives AHU shutdown, dampers, lifts and door release together.

  6. Week 0

    Handover

    As-builts, O&M manuals, staff training and 12 months of defects cover.
