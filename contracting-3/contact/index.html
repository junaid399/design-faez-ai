Questions we hear on the first call.

If yours is not here, the estimating line is the quickest way to an answer.

Do you work outside the Eastern Province?

Occasionally, for existing clients with a branch network. For single projects in Riyadh or Jeddah we usually recommend a local MEP contractor, because aftercare matters more than the price.

Can you price from a design that is not finished?

Yes. We give a budget range from concept drawings, list the assumptions, and firm up the price once the design is frozen. Many clients use that range to set their own approval budget.

Do you design the MEP or only build it?

Both. Our engineers produce design and build MEP to IFC level, or we build from a consultant's design and produce the shop drawings and coordination model.

Who handles Civil Defense and municipality approvals?

We do, for fire protection and fit-out permits. We are a Civil Defense approved class A fire contractor, so submissions go in under our own licence.

What warranty do we get?

Twelve months defects liability on every project, manufacturers' warranties passed to you in the O&M manual, and an optional maintenance plan from day one of handover.

Can you work at night in an occupied building?

Most of our mall, bank and hotel work is done at night or at weekends. We plan noise, dust and shutdowns with your facilities team before work starts.
