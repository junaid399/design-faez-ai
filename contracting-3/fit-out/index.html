The finishes we specify most, and why.

Eastern Province buildings face salt air, sand and 50 °C roof temperatures. These eight materials survive that, are made or stocked in the Kingdom, and age well under heavy use.

    Slatted walls and joinery. FSC certified, fire rated to class B.

Cat A or Cat B? Usually both.

Landlords call us for Cat A to make a floor lettable. Tenants call us for Cat B to make it theirs. When one contractor does both, nothing gets ripped out between them.

A

Cat A: for the landlord

A clean, lettable floor with services ready to be adapted.

  • Raised floor and suspended ceiling
  • HVAC to an open-plan layout
  • Base lighting and small power grid
  • Sprinklers and fire alarm to open plan
  • Toilets and core finishes

Typical: 6 to 10 weeks per floor

+
B

Cat B: for the tenant

The workplace itself, designed around how the team works.

  • Partitions, meeting rooms, glazing
  • HVAC and lighting re-zoned to the layout
  • Pantries, prayer rooms, server rooms
  • Joinery, flooring, furniture, signage
  • AV, access control and IT cabling

Typical: 10 to 16 weeks per floor
