Fresh is not the same as ready.

Freshly roasted beans are full of carbon dioxide, which blocks extraction and makes the cup taste sharp and hollow. That is why our bags rest seven days before they reach the shelf.

  • Keep the bag sealed and squeeze the air out. The valve lets gas out, not oxygen in.
  • No fridge. Riyadh kitchens are dry; condensation is the enemy, not heat.
  • Grind just before brewing. Ground coffee fades in about 20 minutes.

Before you order.

Can you grind it for my brewer?

Yes. Tell us the brewer in your enquiry or at the bar and we grind it on our EK43 for V60, AeroPress, French press, moka pot or Turkish. Whole bean keeps longer, though.

How does delivery work in Riyadh?

We deliver on Mondays and Thursdays across Riyadh. Free over two bags, SAR 15 otherwise. Outside Riyadh we ship with SMSA, usually in two working days.

What does omni roast mean?

A roast that works for both filter and espresso. Slightly more developed than our filter roasts, still light enough to keep the origin character.

Why is the Yemeni coffee so expensive?

Yields in Haraz are tiny, the terraces are hand worked, and we pay nearly five times the market price. It is also one of the rarest coffees in the world.

Do you sell green coffee for home roasting?

In 1 kg bags at the Al Malqa bar, for any lot on the shelf. Ask the roaster on Sunday or Wednesday mornings.

Buying by the kilo for a café?

Wholesale starts at 10 kg a month with free recipe setup and barista training at our lab.
