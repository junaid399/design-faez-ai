Green to bag in nine minutes and forty seconds.

Every batch is logged. This is batch 412, our Ethiopia Guji, on the 15 kg drum.

Development 1:30, 15.5% of the roast

01

Green arrives

Lots land at Jeddah port in GrainPro bags and rest in our store at 18°C and 55% humidity. We sample roast every lot before we commit.

Moisture 10.8% / density 0.74 g/ml

02

Charge

Fifteen kilos go into a drum preheated to 200°C. Light roasts need energy early, then a gentle hand once the beans turn yellow.

Charge 200°C / gas 80%

03

Listen for first crack

The beans pop like popcorn at about 196°C. From here every second changes the cup, so the roaster pulls the trier and smells, not just reads the screen.

First crack 8:10 / 196°C

04

Drop and cool

We drop at 207°C and the cooling tray brings the batch to room temperature in under four minutes, which locks in the sweetness.

Drop 9:40 / weight loss 13.2%

05

Cup, then bag

Every batch is cupped next morning against the reference. If it does not match, it becomes staff coffee. Then into valve bags with the roast date stamped.

Cupped Thursday 7:30 am
