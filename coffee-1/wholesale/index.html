More than bags at the back door.

What every wholesale account gets from the first order, whatever the volume.

01

Your recipe, set on day one

A roaster comes with a scale and a refractometer, dials in your grinder for our coffee and leaves the recipe card on your bar. Dose, yield, time, temperature.

02

Monthly quality visit

We taste your shots, check extraction and adjust for the new roast. You get a short report on WhatsApp.

03

Lab training

Up to eight hours a month for your baristas at our Al Malqa lab: milk, dialling in, cupping, workflow.

04

Roast to door in 48 hours

Order by Saturday or Tuesday night. Your coffee is roasted the next morning and delivered the day after.

05

Machine care partners

Certified technicians for espresso machines and grinders, with a 24 hour response for our partners.

06

Menu cards in two languages

Tasting notes and origin cards for your menu, printed in Arabic and English, refreshed with each new lot.

No contract, no machine lock-in.

Questions owners ask us.

Do we have to use your machine?
No. We work with whatever you have and only suggest changes when the grinder is holding the coffee back.
Can we put our own name on the bags?
From the Partner tier we roast a house blend under your name, with our roast date and origin data on the label.
What if we run out mid-week?
Partners can call for an emergency 5 kg from our stock bags, delivered the same day inside Riyadh.
Do you supply outside Riyadh?
Yes, to Jeddah, Dammam and Al Khobar by chilled courier, two days after roasting. Minimum 25 kg a month.
