Grind decides almost everything.

Finer grinds extract faster, coarser grinds slower. Match the grind to how long the water touches the coffee. Sizes are average particle size in microns.

  1. Turkish100 µm, powder
  2. Espresso250 µm, fine sand
  3. V60600 µm, table salt
  4. AeroPress700 µm
  5. Chemex900 µm, coarse sand
  6. French press1,100 µm
  7. Cold brew1,400 µm, sea salt

Fix your cup in one change.

Taste first, then move one thing at a time. Sour means too little was extracted, bitter means too much.

Tastes sourGrind finer

Or use water a few degrees hotter.

Tastes bitterGrind coarser

Or shorten the brew by 20 seconds.

Tastes thinAdd 1 g of coffee

Keep the water the same.

Tastes heavyAdd 20 g of water

Strength drops, flavour stays.

Coffee is 98% water. Riyadh tap is not your friend.

Our tap water is hard and changes week to week. Use a bottled water with 75 to 150 ppm of dissolved minerals, the number printed as TDS on the label.

DistilledBrew hereTypical tap
0100200300400 ppm
  • Too soft (under 50 ppm): flat, sour, hollow.
  • Just right (75 to 150 ppm): sweet and clear.
  • Too hard (over 250 ppm): chalky, muted, scale in your kettle.
