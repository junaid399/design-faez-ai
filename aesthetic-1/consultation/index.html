Can I choose my doctor?

Yes. Name them in the form. If they are fully booked we offer the earliest slot with a colleague who shares the same focus.

Is the consultation covered by insurance?

Medical dermatology (acne, eczema, moles) is covered by most major insurers with direct billing. Aesthetic treatments are self-pay.

Can I have treatment the same day?

Peels, injectables and some lasers can be done the same day if safe. Lasers on darker skin types wait for a 48-hour patch test.

Do you see men?

Yes, about a third of our patients are men, mostly for acne scars, hair loss and razor bumps, on a separate floor.

Can I send photos first?

Please do, by WhatsApp. A doctor will say whether it needs a consultation, a dermatology visit or a GP.

What if I need to cancel?

Free changes up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours the fee stays as credit for your next visit.

Before you come

  • National ID or Iqama, and insurance card for medical visits
  • Names of creams and medicines you use, a photo of the labels is enough
  • Come without makeup on the area you want examined
  • Old photos if the concern changes with season or cycle
