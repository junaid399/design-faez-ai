Before and after, in plain words.

You receive these instructions in writing and by WhatsApp in Arabic or English after booking.

Before your session

Stop retinoids and exfoliating acids

Five days before lasers, peels and needling. Moisturiser and sunscreen are fine to continue.

Avoid sun and tanning for two weeks

Tanned skin absorbs more laser energy at the surface. We may postpone if your skin has darkened since the consultation.

Tell us about cold sores or new medicines

Anyone with a history of cold sores receives antiviral cover before resurfacing. Some antibiotics make skin light-sensitive.

Arrive with clean skin

No makeup on the area. Numbing cream is applied at the clinic 30 to 45 minutes before needling and lasers.

After your session

Expect warmth and redness

Like mild sunburn for a few hours after toning, up to five days after CO2. Cool compresses help; ice directly on skin does not.

Sunscreen every three hours outdoors

Mineral SPF 50 for four weeks. In Riyadh summer we recommend a hat and shade even for short walks.

No gym, sauna or hammam for 48 hours

Heat and sweat increase swelling and irritation after energy treatments and injectables.

When to call us

Blisters, spreading redness, severe pain or any change in vision after injections. The on-call doctor line is open until midnight.

Not sure which row is yours?

That is what the consultation is for. Forty minutes, a dermatoscope, a written plan with prices. The fee is credited to your first treatment.
