Six wavelengths, one rule: the device follows the diagnosis.

A laser is a single colour of light, and each colour is absorbed by a different target: pigment, blood, water or the hair bulb. We keep six so that we never stretch one machine to fit every patient.

Five checks before any energy touches your skin

SFDA · MDMA registered

All eleven devices are registered with the Saudi Food and Drug Authority, serviced every quarter by the manufacturer, and logged per patient. Ask to see the log for your session.

  1. 1

    Diagnosis and skin type

    Dermatoscope assessment and Fitzpatrick typing by the consultant, recorded in your file.

  2. 2

    Standard photographs

    Same light, same distance, three angles, so progress is measured rather than remembered.

  3. 3

    Patch test

    Lasers and peels on skin types IV to VI are test-spotted and checked after 48 hours.

  4. 4

    Written consent

    Risks, expected downtime and alternatives in Arabic or English, signed before day one.

  5. 5

    48-hour review call

    A nurse calls two days after every energy treatment. Concerns go straight to your doctor.
