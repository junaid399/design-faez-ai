Why our photographs can be compared.

Lighting alone can make pigment look 30% lighter. So every photograph at Noor is taken the same way, in the same room, by the same trained nurse.

  • 5fixed camera positions on a floor template, 1.2 m from the patient
  • 2daylight softboxes at 5500 K, room lights off
  • 1cross-polarised image for pigment and vessels, one standard image for texture
  • 0makeup, filters or retouching
