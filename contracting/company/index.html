Twenty-eight years of handing over on time.

Rukn started in 1998 with forty people and one transit mixer in Al Malaz. It is still owned by the family that founded it, and still run from Riyadh.

  2. 1998

    Founded in Al Malaz

    Eng. Abdulrahman Al-Qahtani opens Rukn with 40 staff and a single transit mixer.

  3. 2004

    First hospital

    A 90-bed general hospital in Al Kharj, handed over in 22 months.

  4. 2009

    Grade 1 classification

    Classified Grade 1 for buildings, opening the door to government tenders of any size.

  5. 2013

    Own concrete supply

    First batching plant commissioned in Riyadh’s Second Industrial City.

  6. 2018

    ISO 45001

    Certified to the new occupational health and safety standard in its first year.

  7. 2021

    Jeddah office

    A permanent western region team and a second batching plant.

  8. 2023

    Dammam office and MEP division

    An Eastern Province base and a 600-strong MEP division of our own.

  9. 2026

    14 active sites

    3,860 people on site today, 41% of them Saudi nationals.
