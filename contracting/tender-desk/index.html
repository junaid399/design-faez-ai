Procurement questions

The questions we are asked most often at prequalification.

Do you provide bid and performance bonds?

Yes. Bonds are issued by Saudi banks, typically a 2% bid bond and a 10% performance bond, and we can match the wording in your conditions of contract.

Can you provide a local content certificate?

Yes. Our current Local Content and Government Procurement Authority certificate is included in the pack, together with the baseline score behind it.

What payment terms do you work to?

Monthly interim valuations certified by the consultant, paid within 30 to 60 days, with 10% advance payment against an advance payment guarantee.

Do you work as a subcontractor to international contractors?

Yes, for civil and MEP packages on giga-project sites, under main contractor HSE and quality systems as well as our own.

How quickly can you mobilise?

Usually three to six weeks from award, depending on site access, permits and whether a batching plant is needed on site.

Can we visit an active site before shortlisting?

Yes. Ask for a visit in the request form and we will arrange a site of similar scope with the project manager on hand.
