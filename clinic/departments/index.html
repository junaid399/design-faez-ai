Tests done downstairs, results the same day.

The laboratory and imaging suite sit on the ground floor, so most tests happen during your visit. These are our usual turnaround times.

Hospital equipment, clinic waiting times.

Digital X-ray

Low-dose digital images, read by a radiologist and shared with your doctor before you leave the consultation room.

Ultrasound

Pregnancy, abdominal and thyroid scans, with a woman sonographer on request.

Dental imaging

Panoramic and 3D scans for implants, braces and wisdom teeth, done in the dental suite.

Second opinions and referrals.

Bring the reports and scans you already have. If you need hospital care, we send your file ahead and book the appointment with you.

  • We review outside reports and imaging at no extra charge
  • Referrals to Riyadh hospitals for surgery and admission
  • Reports explained in Arabic or English
