A visit that leaves you your evening.

Most appointments take under an hour from the car park and back. This is how the time is spent.

45min typical visit, door to door
  1. Before you arrive

    Book online or by phone

    Choose a doctor and a time window. We confirm by SMS within 30 minutes.

  2. About 5 min

    Check in at the kiosk

    Scan your ID or Iqama. Insurance is verified while you take a seat.

  3. 20 min with your doctor

    See your doctor

    Consultations are booked in 20-minute slots, so nobody hurries you out.

  4. Results within 4 hours

    Results in the app

    Lab results and your prescription arrive in the Nabd app and by SMS.

Direct billing with your insurer

Show your insurance card with your national ID or Iqama at check-in. We confirm your cover before the consultation starts.
