Walk to the end of the plateau

Jebel Fihrayn sits on the western lip of the Tuwaiq escarpment, a limestone wall that runs 800 km through Najd. From the trailhead it is a short, rocky walk down into a saddle and up onto the rim, then along the edge to the point everyone photographs.

  • Sunset from the rim, timed by your guide to the minute
  • Fossil shells in the limestone from when this was sea floor
  • Off-road drive across the Al Hasi plain in Land Cruisers
  • Dinner at the cars: chicken kabsa, dates and Saudi coffee

The day,
hour by hour

Times shown for late October. We shift the start by up to an hour through the season so the walk always ends on the rim at sunset.

  2. 13:30

    Pickup in Riyadh

    From our office in Al Mohammadiyah or your hotel in the north of the city. Water, snacks and the safety briefing in the car.

  3. 15:00

    Off the asphalt at Al Hasi

    Tyres down to 18 psi, then 40 minutes of track across the plain under the cliffs.

  4. 15:40

    Trailhead

    Poles, head torches and a look at the route. Toilets are at the car park only.

  5. 16:00

    Down to the saddle and up to the rim

    The only steep part: 130 metres of climbing on rocky steps, with stops to look at fossil shells.

  6. 17:20

    Jebel Fihrayn for sunset

    Sunset at 17:35 in late October. Your guide takes the group photo at the point and keeps everyone a safe two metres from the edge.

  7. 18:10

    Back by head torch

    The same path back in the blue hour, the city glow on the horizon.

  8. 19:00

    Dinner at the cars

    Chicken kabsa, salads, dates and Saudi coffee on rugs, under the first stars.

  9. 21:30

    Back in Riyadh

    Drop-off at the office or your hotel.

Included

  • Two guides, one a Wilderness First Responder
  • Land Cruiser transport from Riyadh and back
  • Dinner, snacks and 3 litres of water each
  • Walking poles and head torches
  • Photos from the guide, sent the next day

Bring

  • Trainers or boots with grip, no sandals
  • A warm layer: the rim is windy after sunset
  • Sunglasses, cap and sunscreen
  • National ID, iqama or passport

Not suitable for children under 10, or anyone with vertigo or knee problems on steep descents. Ask us about the easier plateau walk instead.
