The boring part
we take seriously

The desert is beautiful and it does not forgive. These are the rules every Rihla trip runs on, written by guides and checked by our operations lead before each departure.

1 guide for every 8 walkers. Two guides on every group departure, whatever the size.

  1. 01
    Wilderness First Responder certification for every guide, renewed every two years.
  2. 02
    Satellite messenger and live tracking in every vehicle, watched from Riyadh until you are back.
  3. 03
    Weather call 48 hours out. Dust storm or a forecast above 38°C and we move the date, free.
  4. 04
    Two vehicles minimum beyond the asphalt, with recovery gear and 20 litres of water each.
