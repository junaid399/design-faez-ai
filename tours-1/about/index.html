من نحنEST. 2016, RIYADH

One Land Cruiser and
a map with holes in it.

Rihla began as weekend trips for friends. Faisal Al Harbi, then a geology graduate working in Riyadh, kept getting asked to repeat the drive to the Tuwaiq escarpment, so he started charging for the fuel.

When tourist visas opened in 2019 the friends of friends became strangers from 40 countries, and the cool box became a kitchen trailer. Today we are 11 guides, four planners and a fleet of six vehicles, still in the same Al Mohammadiyah office.

What has not changed: we only sell trips our own guides have walked, we keep groups small, and we would rather move your date than take you out in bad weather.

Four rules we
do not bend

They cost us bookings sometimes. They are also why most of our travellers come back for a second region.

12

Small groups

Never more than 12 travellers on a group departure, and always two guides. The desert is quieter that way, and so are the photos.

68%

Money stays local

Of every riyal you pay, 68% goes to people in the region you visit: drivers in AlUla, cooks in Al Hasi, guesthouses in Rijal Almaa.

1.2 t

Leave it cleaner

Every group carries out its rubbish and one extra bag. In 2025 our travellers collected 1.2 tonnes from campsites and trailheads.

48 h

Weather comes first

We make the go or no-go call 48 hours before departure. If we move your trip, you choose a new date or a full refund.

How a new trip
gets into the catalogue

On average seven months pass between a guide's idea and the first paying departure. Two ideas in three never make it.

  2. Month 1 to 201

    Two recce visits

    Two guides walk the route in the hardest month we would run it, logging water, shade, signal and exits.

  3. Month 302

    Safety plan

    Evacuation points, nearest hospital, vehicle limits and a weather rule written for that route.

  4. Month 4 to 503

    Test run

    Office staff and their families go as guests and fill in the same feedback form you will.

  5. Month 6 to 704

    First departure

    A half-price launch group, led by the guide who scouted it. Then it gets a code.
