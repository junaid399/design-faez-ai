What is included in the price?

Guide, transport from Riyadh or the regional airport, park and site tickets, meals on the trip, camping gear on overnight trips and water. Flights to AlUla, the Red Sea and Asir are quoted separately so you can use your own miles.

Can I bring children?

Grade 1 and 2 trips welcome children from 5 years. Grade 3 from 10 years with a parent. For younger children we suggest a private trip so we can set the pace.

Do visitors on an eVisa need anything else?

No extra permits for these trips. Bring your passport on the day; some sites check it at the gate. We arrange AlUla and Hegra tickets in your name.

What happens if the weather turns?

We make the call 48 hours before departure. If we postpone for dust, rain in the wadis or heat, you choose a new date or a full refund.

How fit do I need to be?

Check the grade. As a guide: if you can walk an hour at a steady pace, grades 1 to 3 are comfortable. For grade 4 and 5 we call you before booking to talk through your experience.

A birthday on the escarpment, a proposal at Elephant Rock, a school trip to Ushaiger. Tell us the idea and the dates; we send a plan and a price within one working day.
