Before you send the form.

Is registering a commitment?

No. It only puts you on the priority list for price lists, releases and site walks. You can ask us to remove your details at any time.

Do I pay anything now?

Nothing. The first payment is the 10% reservation, made only after you have chosen a plot and signed the contract.

Can I register for Phase 3?

Yes. Mention West Ridge in your message and we will send its plot map before the 2027 release.
