What does it mean that the project is Wafi licensed?

Wafi is the government programme that licenses off-plan sales in Saudi Arabia. A licensed project must hold buyer payments in a dedicated escrow account, appoint an independent consultant and an auditor, and can only draw money against certified progress.

How much do I pay to reserve a villa?

10% of the villa price on signing the sale contract. That amount goes to the escrow account. The next instalment is due only when foundations for your phase are certified.

Can I use a mortgage?

Yes. Several Saudi banks finance Wafi off-plan villas, usually from the handover instalment. Our advisers can introduce you to bank officers who already know the project.

When will I receive the keys and the title deed?

Phase 1 handover is planned for Q4 2027, Phase 2 for Q3 2028 and Phase 3 for 2029. The title deed is transferred at handover after the final instalment.

Is there a service charge?

An owners' association runs the parks, security and street cleaning. The estimated charge is SAR 9 per m² of built-up area a year, fixed for the first two years.

Can I change the layout or finishes?

You can choose between two finish palettes and, before structure starts on your plot, move some internal walls on the upper floor. Facades stay as designed to keep the streets consistent.
