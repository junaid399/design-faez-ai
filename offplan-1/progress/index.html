The photo log, month by month.

Taken from the same consultant visits that unlock each escrow payment.

  1. Jun 2025Report 01

    Ground broken

    Site cleared and levelled. Excavation for the first 20 Phase 1 villas begins after the soil report is approved.

  2. Sep 2025Report 04

    First concrete

    Blinding and the first raft pours. Every pour is sampled and cube-tested by an accredited lab.

  3. Nov 2025Report 06

    Foundations certified

    All 80 Phase 1 foundations complete. The consultant certifies the stage and the second instalment falls due.

  4. Feb 2026Report 09

    Ground floors rise

    Columns and first-floor slabs across East Rise. Up to six crews pour in parallel, mornings only in summer.

  5. Apr 2026Report 11

    Topped out

    First 40 structures topped out. Block work follows street by street, starting next to the park.

  6. Jul 2026Report 14

    All roofs closed

    Structure complete on all 80 villas. The third instalment is certified for Phase 1 owners.

  7. Sep 2026Report 16

    Inside the shells

    Ducts, pipes and cable trays go in. Owners on the September site walk saw their own rooms for the first time.

  8. Oct 2026Report 17, latest

    Ready for stone

    Limestone cladding starts on the street facades of East Rise. Phase 2 piling is 46% done.
