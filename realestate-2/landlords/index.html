How we let your home, week by week

From the first visit to the monthly statement. You approve the tenant and the contract on your phone, wherever you are.

  1. Day 1

    Valuation and condition check

    An agent visits, tests the AC, water and electrics, and sets a rent from our last 12 months of lets in your district.

  2. Day 3

    Photos, floor plan, listing

    Our photographer shoots in daylight. The listing goes live on Bayt, Aqar and our WhatsApp list of waiting families.

  3. Days 4 to 20

    Viewings, evenings included

    We host every viewing and send you a short note after each one, with the family's questions and feedback.

  4. Within 48 hours of an offer

    Tenant checks

    ID or Iqama, employer letter and Ejar rental history. You see the summary and approve or decline.

  5. Signing day

    Ejar contract and move-in report

    The unified contract is registered on Ejar. A dated photo report of every room protects your deposit claim later.

  6. Every month

    Rent, repairs, statement

    Rent arrives through Ejar. Repairs are quoted to you before work starts. A statement lands on the 1st.
