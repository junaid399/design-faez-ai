From first viewing to keys, in five steps on Ejar.

Ejar إيجار is the national rental platform. Your contract, payments and deposit are recorded there, so nothing depends on a handshake.

  1. Day 0

    View

    In person or on a video call. You get the inspection report before you go.

  2. Day 1

    Reserve

    A refundable SAR 2,000 hold, credited to your first payment.

  3. Day 2 to 3

    Sign on Ejar

    We draft the unified contract. You and the owner approve it by SMS.

  4. Day 4

    First payment

    Annual, two or four instalments, paid through the Ejar SADAD number.

  5. Day 9, typical

    Keys

    A joint move-in check with photos and meter readings, then the keys.

Ask on WhatsApp
Can I rent before my Iqama is issued?

Yes. Many relocating families sign with a passport and visa, then we update the Ejar record once the Iqama arrives. Your employer's letter helps the owner say yes faster.

How do the rent payments work?

Most owners accept one, two or four payments a year. Each instalment gets a SADAD number through Ejar, so you pay from any Saudi bank app and the record is automatic.

What does it cost me as a tenant?

A brokerage fee of 2.5% of one year's rent, the Ejar contract fee, and a refundable deposit, usually 5% of the annual rent. No viewing fees, ever.

Are homes furnished?

About a third of our apartments are furnished. Villas usually come with kitchen appliances and AC units only. Filter by furnished on the rentals page.

Who handles repairs?

Structural, AC and plumbing faults are the owner's job unless you caused them. On managed homes our team sends the technician and settles the bill with the owner.

Can I leave before the lease ends?

Every Bayt contract states the notice period, usually 60 days, and what is owed if you leave early. We negotiate a job relocation clause where we can.

Tell us the district and the move‑in date.

We will line up three viewings that fit, usually within 48 hours. Evenings and Saturdays included.
