Bring these and you can reserve on the day

Nothing is signed at a viewing, but with these ready we can hold the home for you the same evening.

  1. 01
    National ID, Iqama or passport

    For the Ejar record. A passport and visa is fine if your Iqama is still being issued.

  2. 02
    Employer letter or salary certificate

    Owners ask for it on homes above SAR 100,000 a year.

  3. 03
    A Saudi mobile number

    Ejar sends the approval code by SMS. Ask us if you do not have one yet.

  4. 04
    Your payment preference

    One, two or four instalments. It changes which owners will say yes.
