Questions owners ask first.

Anything else, ask in the form below or call our partnerships lead on 920 018 844.

Who owns the recipe and the brand?

You do. We sign a recipe confidentiality agreement before the first tasting, and the brand name, logo and app pages stay registered to you.

How does the revenue share work?

After the app commission, we split net sales per order. The split depends on food cost and is fixed in the contract before launch, with no monthly minimum.

Can my brand stay on my own app pages too?

Yes. If you already have listings, we move them to our kitchen's location so your ratings and followers come with you.

What if the dish does not travel well?

We ride every item for 40 minutes in a real delivery box before launch. If something goes soggy we change the build or the packaging, not your flavours.

How long is the contract?

Twelve months, with a 90 day review. Either side can leave at the review with 30 days' notice.

Do you take brands that need special equipment?

Sometimes. A charcoal grill or a tandoor needs its own station and longer planning, so tell us early in the form.
