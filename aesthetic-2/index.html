Letter from the founder

We built a house,not a clinic.

When I opened Velour in 2015, every aesthetic clinic in Jeddah looked the same: bright corridors, a television in the waiting room, a menu of forty treatments and a price list for packages of ten. Women came in rushed and left the same way.

So we did the opposite. Four private suites in an old villa on the Corniche, a separate women's entrance, one client per practitioner per hour, and a menu that changes with the weather. Our city has seasons, even if visitors think it does not. Skin feels every one of them.

This issue is our autumn: the season for repair after a long, bright summer. Read it slowly. Then come and sit with us for an hour and a half.

Jeddah has seasons.So does skin.

From May to August the UV index here peaks at 12 nearly every day and humidity rarely drops below half. December brings it down to 7. We plan the whole year around that curve, so the treatment you book suits the light you will walk out into.

  1. Dec to FebWinter: NourishBarrier repair while the sun is gentlest. The season for deeper peels.
  2. Mar to MaySpring: RenewResurfacing and skin boosters before the heat arrives.
  3. Jun to AugSummer: DefendHydration, calming and pigment control. No aggressive peels.
  4. Sep to NovAutumn: RepairUndoing the summer: tone, texture and the first retinoids. In season now.

Bars: peak UV index by month, Jeddah, ten year average. Outer ring: the collection on our menu that month.

The ritual

Ninety minutes,accounted for.

Every first visit follows the same five movements. The treatment itself is only half of it. The rest is what makes the treatment work.

0 to 10
10 to 25
25 to 65
65 to 80
80 to 90

0 min90 min

  1. Arrive 10 min

    Private entrance, cardamom tea, phone into a linen pouch.

  2. Read 15 min

    Your practitioner looks at your skin under daylight and a lamp, and asks about your summer.

  3. Treat 40 min

    The treatment itself, in your suite, at the pace your skin allows. Nothing is added on the day without asking.

  4. Still 15 min

    Fifteen minutes under a warm towel with the lights down. Nobody comes in.

  5. Carry 10 min

    A written aftercare card for the next seven days, and the date your skin is ready again.
