Before youcome in.

  1. 01

    Come with a clean face

    Or with your usual make up; we have everything to remove it. Just no fresh self tan.

  2. 02

    Bring your routine

    A photo of your products is enough. It saves ten minutes of guessing at the reading.

  3. 03

    Pause the strong things

    Retinoids and acids for five days before a peel or microneedling. Your confirmation message lists what applies to you.

  4. 04

    Arrive ten minutes early

    For tea in the lounge. Your suite is ready when you are; nobody is rushed out after.

  5. 05

    Plan the evening

    After most treatments you can go straight to dinner. After peels and boosters, plan a quiet night in.

We will have the tea ready, the lights low and your name on the door.
