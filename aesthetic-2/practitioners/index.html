Founder and medical director Since 2015

Dr. Hala Al Harbi

Consultant dermatologist. Trained at King Abdulaziz University Hospital, with a fellowship in pigment disorders. Sees the hard cases: melasma, scarring, skin that has been treated badly elsewhere.

What do you say no to most often?
Lip filler for twenty year olds who want someone else's mouth. I would rather sell them a lip balm and see them again in ten years.
The treatment you would never do in August?
A medium depth peel. Our sun would undo it in a week and leave pigment behind.

Aesthetic physician Since 2021

Dr. Lujain Bakr

Boosters, polynucleotides, soft tox and lip hydration. Known among members for doing less than they asked for, and for being right about it.

What does "light hands" mean?
Two units fewer than you think. You can always add at the two week review. You cannot take away.
What brings people back?
Their mother noticing they look rested, and not being able to say why.

Lead aesthetician Since 2017

Noor Al Sharif

Wrote the Velour Facial and still rewrites it every season. CIDESCO certified, fourteen years in treatment rooms from Beirut to Jeddah.

Why twenty minutes of massage?
Because humidity makes the face hold water. Ten minutes moves it. Twenty changes the shape of the jaw for a week.
Your own routine, honestly?
Oil cleanse, a good moisturiser, sunscreen I actually reapply. And sleep, which nobody sells.

Skin therapist Since 2022

Rawan Qadi

Microneedling, LED and the sensitive skin clinic on Tuesday evenings. The person to see if every product makes you red.

What is a sensitive skin clinic?
One hour, fewer products, a patch test on the jaw, and a lot of questions about what you use at home.
Most common mistake you see?
Three acids in one routine because each one was recommended by a different person online.
