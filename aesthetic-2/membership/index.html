The small things members mention most

Notes that follow you

Bring someone

The terms, without the small print

Starting
After your first skin reading, on the first day of any month. No joining fee.
Leaving
One month's notice by message or phone. No minimum term after the first three months.
Pausing
For travel, pregnancy or illness, up to the months your tier allows, with no fee.
Unused treatments
They do not roll over. If we could not find you a slot, we add one to the next month.
Sharing
Your treatments are yours. Guest passes are the only way to share.
Price changes
At most once a year, with two months' notice. Members who joined before keep their price for twelve months.
