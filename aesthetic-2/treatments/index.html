Book the Velour Facial

Four rules we keep,even when asked not to.

I

We will say no

If a treatment will not give you the result you want, or will look wrong on your face, your practitioner will tell you and suggest something else. About one request in eight ends this way.

II

Pregnancy pauses injectables

No neuromodulators, fillers, boosters or medical peels while pregnant or breastfeeding. Facials, lymphatic work and LED are all fine, and we adjust products for you.

III

Two weeks before an event

Nothing new within fourteen days of a wedding or a photograph you care about. Book your last treatment early and we will hold a calming facial for the week itself.

IV

Tell us about retinoids

Stop prescription retinoids five days before any peel or microneedling. Isotretinoin in the last six months rules out most of the menu; we will plan around it.
