Questions we hear every day.

Something else? Our coordinators answer the phone and WhatsApp from 7:30 am.

Message a coordinator
Do I need a referral letter to book?

Not for a consultant appointment. For MRI and CT, insurers usually ask for one; if you do not have it, our consultant can see you first and request the scan the same day.

Can you read scans that were done somewhere else?

Yes. Bring the CD or send a download link. We re-report outside MRI and CT studies, and the consultant compares them with any new images we take.

I am nervous about the MRI tunnel. What can you do?

Our magnet has a 70 cm bore, wider than most, and many scans are done feet first so your head stays outside. A family member can sit in the room with you after a safety check.

Will the report be in Arabic?

The formal report is in English, as your doctor will expect. Every report comes with a one-page summary in Arabic written for you, not for another doctor.

Do women patients see women staff?

Ask when you book. Women radiographers and sonographers are on every shift, and we have women consultants in cardiology and radiology.

How do I pay if I am not insured?

You get a written price before booking. We take cash, cards and bank transfer, and the price includes the report and a copy of your images.
