Cardiology

Consultant cardiologists who do their own echocardiograms, so the person who scans your heart is the person who explains it.

What we see most

  • Chest pain and tightness
  • Palpitations and fainting
  • High blood pressure
  • Breathlessness and swollen ankles
  • Heart murmurs
  • Risk checks before surgery or sport

Orthopaedics, surgery last.

Knee, shoulder, hip and spine problems are assessed with MRI on the same day when it is needed. Our surgeons operate at partner hospitals in Muscat, and most of our patients never need them to.

Thursday afternoons, every difficult case gets a room full of opinions.

When a scan does not match the symptoms, or surgery is on the table, your case is presented at the weekly review. Cardiologists, surgeons and radiologists look at the same images together and agree one written plan.

Not sure which clinic you need?
