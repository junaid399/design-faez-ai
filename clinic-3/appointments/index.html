Request an appointment.

For a consultant, a scan or a second opinion. Send the form and a coordinator calls you back, usually within two working hours.

  1. Within 2 h
    A coordinator calls you

    We confirm the right consultant or scan and offer the first slots.

  2. Within 24 h
    Insurance pre-approval

    We request it for you and tell you the outcome, or give a written self-pay price.

  3. Same day
    Confirmation on WhatsApp

    Date, time, preparation notes and a map pin, in English or Arabic.

  4. Your visit
    Arrive 20 minutes early

    Bring your ID or resident card, insurance card and any previous scans.

What to bring, so nothing waits.

i.

For everyone

  • Civil ID or resident card
  • A list of your medicines and allergies
  • Glasses or hearing aids you use for reading forms
  • A family member, if you would like one with you
ii.

If you are insured

  • Your insurance card, front and back
  • The pre-approval number we sent you
  • Your employer letter, for some company plans
iii.

About referral letters

You do not need one to see our consultants. Insurers usually ask for one before MRI or CT; if you do not have it, our consultant can see you first and write the request on the same day.
