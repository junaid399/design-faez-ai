What to bring.

Have these ready and a reservation takes about twenty minutes.

01To reserve

  • National ID, or Iqama and passport
  • Saudi mobile number in your name
  • Bank card or transfer for the 10% deposit

02To sign the contract

  • National address certificate
  • IBAN for refunds and handover
  • Bank pre-approval, if financing the handover payment
  • Power of attorney, if signing for a family member
