Which way your windows face

The tower plate is turned eight degrees off the street grid so that no living room looks straight into a neighbour.

  • E
    KAFD towers, 600 m

    Morning light and the lit financial district at night. Most Horizon corners face this way.

  • S
    Olaya and the Kingdom Centre, 7 km

    The long view down King Fahd Road. Summit homes wrap this side.

  • W
    Wadi Hanifah escarpment

    The sunset side, with the desert ridge on clear evenings.

  • N
    The airport road and the desert edge

    Quiet, even northern light, chosen by artists and late sleepers.
