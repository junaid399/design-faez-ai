Three metro lines, one walk away

KAFD station is an interchange on the Riyadh Metro. From Qimam it is a level, shaded eight minute walk along the service road and the pedestrian bridge.

  • Line 1South along Olaya to the city centre
  • Line 4North-east to King Khalid International Airport
  • Line 6East across the northern districts
