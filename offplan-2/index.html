Where your money waits

Qimam is sold under Wafi, the off-plan sales programme of the Ministry of Municipalities and Housing. Every riyal you pay is held in an escrow account for this tower alone.

01You pay the instalmentStraight into the project escrow account, never to the developer
02The bank holds itOne account per project, audited every quarter
03The consultant certifiesAn independent engineer signs off each stage on site
04Funds are releasedOnly the certified amount goes to the contractor
