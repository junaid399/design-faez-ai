What protects you as an off-plan buyer

Wafi sets the rules every licensed off-plan project in the Kingdom follows. Before you pay anything you will receive and can check each of these.

  • Wafi licence

    Licence No. 1446/7781, which you can verify with the programme.

  • Escrow account details

    The bank and the account that receive every instalment for this tower.

  • The sale agreement

    Unit number, area, specification, price and handover date in writing.

  • Progress reports

    A quarterly report from the independent consultant with site photographs.

  • Area tolerance

    If the built area differs from the contract, the price is adjusted per square metre.

  • Defects cover

    Ten years on the structure, one year on finishes and systems after handover.

Questions buyers ask first

What does Wafi licensing mean for me?

Wafi is the Kingdom's off-plan sales programme. A licensed project must hold buyers' payments in a dedicated escrow account and can only draw on it against construction stages certified by an independent consultant.

Can I choose my exact floor and view?

Yes. Homes are released level by level, and your adviser shares the live availability list. Higher floors and east-facing corners carry a premium shown on the price list.

What happens if construction runs late?

Your money stays in escrow until each stage is certified. The sale agreement sets out the handover date and the compensation owed for delay beyond the grace period.

Can non-Saudi residents buy?

Eligibility depends on residency status and the current ownership regulations. Your adviser checks each case with our legal team before you reserve.

Can I finance an off-plan home?

Several Saudi banks finance homes in Wafi-licensed projects. We can introduce you to their mortgage teams; the bank sets the terms.

What will the service charge be?

The current estimate is SAR 28 per square metre a year, covering the amenity floors, concierge, security and cooling of shared areas. The owners' association sets the final figure after handover.

When can I see a finished apartment?

A full-size Horizon kitchen and bathroom are in the sales gallery now. The furnished show apartment on Level 40 is planned to open when the facade closes in 2028.
