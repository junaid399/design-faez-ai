Higher is not always better.

We score the view from 1,240 balconies on both coasts. The best seats sit between the 10th and 18th floors: high enough to clear the palms, low enough to still see the beach, the swimmers and the colour of the water.

The golden hour, logged.

Every listing is photographed at its own golden hour. In Jeddah that is the sun dropping into the sea. In Khobar it is the first light coming off the Gulf, and the pink water after sunset.

Buying as a non-Saudi

Living here on an iqama? You can own the view.

The updated rules on non-Saudi ownership opened set zones to foreign residents and companies. We check every listing against the published zones before you book a viewing, so nothing falls apart at the notary.

A note on rental yield

7.4%
