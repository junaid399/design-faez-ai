What our view labels actually mean.

Portals call everything "sea view". We stand on the balcony with a compass and measure how wide the slice of water is.

Full sea
At least 90 degrees of open water from the main living room, with the horizon unbroken.
Side sea
Water seen at an angle, between 30 and 90 degrees, usually from the balcony rather than the sofa.
Creek or marina
Calm, close water: Obhur creek or a marina basin. Boats and evening lights instead of horizon.
