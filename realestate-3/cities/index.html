Al Khobar: the corniche and the bay.

Twelve kilometres of seafront promenade, then the open coast running south to Half Moon Bay. Flats on the corniche, villas a street or two back, chalets on the bay.

  1. 1

    Al Kurnaysh

    The newest towers, the widest promenade. Flats from SAR 1.4M, most with full Gulf views above the 8th floor.

  2. 2

    Corniche, Al Yarmouk

    The water tower end. Cafes, walkers at dawn, and the most liquid resale market on the coast.

  3. 3

    Al Bandariyah

    Villas on quiet streets two blocks back. Side sea from roof terraces, family rentals easy to find.

  4. 4

    Al Aziziyah

    Where the city thins out. A handful of houses right on the rocks, rarely for sale.

  5. 5

    Half Moon Bay

    Chalet country, 30 minutes south. Shallow, calm water and sand you can reach in bare feet.

Jeddah: 32 km from Obhur to Al Hamra.

Read the Jeddah coast from north to south. The further north you go, the newer the homes, the clearer the water and the higher the price.

  1. km 0

    North Obhur

    Private chalets on the creek mouth and open sea. Our most expensive stretch, and the one with the most sunsets over water.

    See the Obhur chalet
  2. km 6

    South Obhur

    Marinas, townhouses with berths and flats over the creek. Calm water, boats instead of horizon.

  3. km 14

    Al Murjan and the North Corniche

    High towers and penthouses. The sweet-spot floors here look over the whole bay to the fountain.

  4. km 22

    Al Shati

    The established corniche: wide balconies, mature palms, a ten-minute walk to the waterfront park.

  5. km 32

    Al Hamra

    Older buildings close to the centre. Smaller sea views, the best value per square metre in the city.

When to come and look.

Average daytime highs on land and at sea. Viewing season, when balconies are pleasant at noon, runs from November to March on both coasts.
