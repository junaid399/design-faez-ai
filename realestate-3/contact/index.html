Questions buyers ask first.

Do you charge buyers a fee?

Our brokerage fee is 2.5% of the price and is agreed in writing before any viewing. On most homes it is split with the seller; we tell you which on the shortlist.

Can I buy as a non-Saudi resident?

In zones open to non-Saudi ownership, yes. Huda on our buyers desk checks your eligibility and the zone of each home before you view it.

Can I view by video first?

Yes. We film from the balcony at noon and at sunset, unedited, and send both before you travel.

How do you score a view?

Two agents rate the width of open water, the horizon, the beach and what blocks it, from the main living room and the balcony. The score is the average, out of ten.

Do you manage chalets for nightly lets?

We introduce owners to two licensed holiday-home operators in Obhur and Half Moon Bay, and share their real booking calendars before you buy.
