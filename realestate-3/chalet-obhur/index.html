Built in 2019 for a family who spent every weekend on the water.

The house turns its back on the road and opens completely to the west. A men's majlis and a guest suite sit by the entrance, so visitors never cross the family side. Everything else steps down towards the sea: living room, pool terrace, lawn, then sand.

Upstairs, four suites share a long shaded balcony. The main suite has its own deck and plunge pool, and the best sunset seat in the house. A boat lift on the creek side takes a 28 ft boat.

The sun sets into the sea every night of the year.

Along the Red Sea the sunset point swings about 50 degrees between winter and summer. Many chalets lose the June sunset behind the headland. This one keeps all of them: its open sea view runs from 230° to 300°.
