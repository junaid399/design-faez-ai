Hittin

Large plots, mostly detached villas built since 2010, and some of the quietest streets in the city. Families move here for space and stay for the schools. Riyadh Front and the Boulevard are a short drive for weekends.

Diplomatic Quarter

A walled, landscaped district on the Wadi Hanifa edge, home to embassies and their staff. Mostly rentals, with low-rise apartments and villas in white Najdi-modern style and running tracks along the valley.

KAFD and Al Aqiq

Apartments for people who want to walk to work. The Riyadh Metro links the financial district to Olaya and the airport, and Al Aqiq next door has the newest duplex villas within a short drive.

Price against rental return

For investors the cheapest square metre is not always the best buy. Each point is a district: further right costs more to buy, higher up earns more rent for every riyal spent.

Al Narjis and Al Sahafa return the most. Hittin and the Diplomatic Quarter hold value but yield less.
