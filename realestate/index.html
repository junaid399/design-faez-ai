From first viewingto the notary

  1. Viewings

    We shortlist three to five homes and show them in one afternoon, with the floor plans and service charges in hand.

  2. Written offer

    Your offer goes to the owner in writing, with the deposit terms and the date you can complete.

  3. Finance approval

    We coordinate the bank valuation and approval so the price you agreed is the price the bank will lend on.

  4. Transfer on Najiz

    Title moves to your name electronically through the Ministry of Justice. We attend with you until the keys change hands.
