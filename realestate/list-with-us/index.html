How we sell

Six steps, usually six to ten weeks from the first visit to the transfer. You always know who is doing what.

  1. Valuation visit

    We inspect the home, measure it and compare it with recent sales on the same streets.

    You share the title deed and any building permits.

  2. REGA advertising licence

    We apply for the ad licence on the REGA platform; no listing goes live without it.

    You sign the brokerage agreement, which states the fee.

  3. Photography and floor plan

    We photograph at dusk and by day and draw a measured plan.

    You approve the listing text before it is published.

  4. Viewings

    We screen buyers for finance first and attend every viewing.

    You choose the days the home can be shown.

  5. Offer and negotiation

    We bring every written offer to you with our advice on it.

    You accept, counter or decline; nothing is agreed on your behalf.

  6. Transfer on Najiz

    We prepare the transfer with the buyer's bank and book the notary.

    You receive the full price before the title moves.
