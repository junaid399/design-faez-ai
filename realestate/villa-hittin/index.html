Built around a shaded courtyard

The house was designed by its owner, an architect, for a family of six. Every main room opens onto a central courtyard planted with olive trees, so the ground floor stays cool and private from the street.

The men's majlis has its own entrance and washroom off the forecourt. The family living room, dining room and kitchen run along the garden side, with sliding glass that opens the whole width to the terrace and pool.

Upstairs are five bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, and a family lounge. The main suite has a dressing room and a balcony over the garden. The roof has a covered sitting area and space for a gym.

Finishes are Italian porcelain, solid oak doors and VRF air conditioning by zone. The villa was completed in 2024 and comes with the remaining structural warranty from the developer.
